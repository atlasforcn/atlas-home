University project

6U CubeSat
embedded-system design

System mode design · Requirements

Developed embedded-system mode design for a 6U CubeSat as a university project, covering state machines, operating modes, functional management and requirements clarification.

I participated in the university project at that stage. The project later became ONGLAISAT, developed further by other professors over the following years. The mission received the 2026 AIAA Small Satellite Mission of the Year Award.
